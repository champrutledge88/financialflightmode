Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Information We Collect

The scorecard calculator runs in your browser. The numbers you enter into the scorecard are used to calculate your score on the page and are not stored by this website.

If you choose EMAIL MY FLIGHT PLAN and check the consent box, we collect your email address and a summary of your Flight Plan so we can email it to you. That summary contains your score, your flight stage, your strongest signal, up to two warning light categories, and the standard plan steps and Starter Kit section that match them. It also records which consent statement you agreed to and when. It never includes the dollar amounts you entered. Those stay in your browser.

If you request the Starter Kit, we collect the email address you submit so we can provide access to the requested resource and send five Pre Flight setup messages over ten days. The sequence ends after message five.

If you separately join The Weekly Briefing, we collect the email address you submit so we can send one issue each week. Joining The Weekly Briefing is not automatic when you request the Starter Kit.

Cockpit

Cockpit reads the bank CSV files you choose to import directly in your browser on your device. Your transactions, balances, category rules, reviews, and medals are saved only in that browser's storage on that device. They are not sent to Financial Flight Mode, MailerLite, Google Analytics, or anyone else, and Financial Flight Mode cannot see them.

Cockpit does not connect to your bank and never asks for bank logins. You can export a backup file, restore it, or erase all Cockpit data at any time from Cockpit Settings. Clearing your browser's website data also erases it.

How We Use Information

We use submitted contact information to provide the specific email experience requested, deliver Starter Kit access, your Flight Plan, or The Weekly Briefing, and improve the experience for visitors.

If you request your Flight Plan by email, we keep your Flight Plan summary with your email signup. When you request it again later, we keep each summary so we can show you how your score and stage change over time. You also agree to receive future Financial Flight Mode education by email, and you can unsubscribe anytime.

We do not sell your personal information.

Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use the website, including page visits and limited funnel events such as starting or completing the Scorecard, viewing a Starter Kit recommendation, completing a signup, requesting a Flight Plan by email, and downloading the Starter Kit. Scorecard usage events also include the question step, derived flight stage and a broad score range. These categories are derived from the result; we do not send an exact score, financial amounts, or your selected pressure and objective.

Financial Flight Mode does not send the financial numbers entered into the Scorecard or your email address to Google Analytics. Analytics may process device, browser, approximate location, referral, campaign, and interaction information and may use cookies or similar technologies according to Google's privacy practices.

Email Provider

Email signup information is processed through MailerLite. MailerLite may process data according to its own privacy and security practices.

Flight Plan summaries and email signup status are also stored in a private customer database hosted by Supabase so Financial Flight Mode can follow your progress over time. This database is not public and does not contain the dollar amounts you enter into the Scorecard.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from Financial Flight Mode emails using the unsubscribe link in any email. You can also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your email signup information and any Flight Plan summaries connected to it.

Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information connected to your email signup, ask for corrections, request deletion, or opt out of certain uses. Financial Flight Mode will respond to valid privacy requests as required by applicable law.

Education Only

Financial Flight Mode provides financial education and planning support. It does not provide personal financial, investment, tax, legal, or debt advice.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact Financial Flight Mode through the email reply path provided in Financial Flight Mode messages.
