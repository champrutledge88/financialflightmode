Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Information We Collect

The scorecard calculator runs in your browser. The numbers you enter into the scorecard are used to calculate your score and Personalized Flight Plan on the page. Raw financial inputs are not stored or transmitted by this website.

If you request the Starter Kit, we collect the email address you submit so we can provide access to the requested resource and send five Pre Flight setup messages over ten days. The sequence ends after message five. Returning subscribers regain resource access without automatically restarting this sequence.

If you separately join The Weekly Briefing, we collect the email address you submit so we can send one issue each week. Joining The Weekly Briefing is not automatic when you request the Starter Kit.

How We Use Information

We use submitted contact information to provide the specific email experience requested, deliver Starter Kit access or The Weekly Briefing, and improve the experience for visitors.

We do not sell your personal information.

Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use the website, including page visits and limited funnel events such as starting or completing the Scorecard, viewing a Starter Kit recommendation, completing a signup, and downloading the Starter Kit.

Financial Flight Mode does not send the financial numbers entered into the Scorecard or your email address to Google Analytics. Personalized plan view events include only categorical stage, signal and decision-path labels. Signup events count successful requests, including repeats; download events count clicks rather than completed workbook setup. Analytics may process device, browser, approximate location, referral, campaign, and interaction information and may use cookies or similar technologies according to Google's privacy practices.

Email Providers and Plan Delivery

If you choose Email My Flight Plan, a separate checkbox asks permission to send that single plan. The request includes your email address, consent version and timestamp, score, stage, approved signal categories, action-library identifier and a random request identifier. Raw financial amounts, ratios, current pressure and objectives are excluded.

Netlify processes this request on our behalf. Resend receives the email address and the approved plan rendered from our fixed action library, including next actions and workbook instructions. Consent version and timestamp are included with the message. Plan delivery does not enroll you in marketing or restart the Starter Kit sequence.

We check your MailerLite email status before sending and do not override paused, unconfirmed, bounced or junk email records. If email delivery is paused, contact us for help. Resend also applies its delivery and suppression controls.

This website does not create a persistent financial profile or store plan requests in a website database. Requests remain in browser memory for retries. Resend retains retry identifiers for 24 hours to prevent duplicate sends; requests expire before that window. Email providers retain delivery content, consent metadata and operational records under their own policies and your account rights. Contact us for access or deletion requests.

Email signup information is processed through MailerLite. MailerLite may process data according to its own privacy and security practices.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from Financial Flight Mode emails using the unsubscribe link in any email. You can also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your email signup information.

Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information connected to your email signup, ask for corrections, request deletion, or opt out of certain uses. Financial Flight Mode will respond to valid privacy requests as required by applicable law.

Education Only

Financial Flight Mode provides financial education and planning support. It does not provide personal financial, investment, tax, legal, or debt advice.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact Financial Flight Mode at hello@financialflightmode.com.
